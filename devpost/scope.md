# On Record

A phone-friendly, installable web app where friends put predictions on record and settle them for points.

## The Unique Kernel

A friendly argument becomes a clear claim that friends commit to before the outcome is known. AI checks their wording, gives the claim a title, and preserves every original position. Settling it changes a shared score.

## Who It's For

Friends who casually disagree about an upcoming event, such as the result of a Manchester derby, and currently leave the claim buried in chat or memory.

## The Core Loop

Someone sends a friend request using a user ID once. After the request is accepted, they choose that friend from their list when adding people to a specific claim. Each person types their own answer or claim. AI checks the wording, finalizes the shared claim and title, and the positions lock. They return when notified to settle the result. Friends compare their scores head to head; group claims also contribute to those comparisons. Profiles earn visible rank-like titles as wins accumulate; more than one friend can hold the same title.

When two people on a group claim are both correct or both incorrect, their head-to-head score does not change. A correct friend gains one point against each friend whose prediction was wrong.

## Inspiration & Identity

The learner wants the energy of friendly bets and the accountability of having the original claim preserved. Visual direction is still open.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

The learner wants this to work as a downloadable web app on a phone if possible, and to make shared predictions more useful than a calendar reminder.

## What "Working" Looks Like

Two account holders can demonstrate one prediction from creation and invitation through locked answers and agreed settlement, with the outcome reflected in their head-to-head score. The same claim flow should also allow more than two participants. The original responses and point change are the visible payoff.

## The POC Boundary

The first version includes account holders, friend requests by user ID, selection of existing friends for each claim, claims with two or more participants, AI checking and titling a shared claim, a due-date notice, mutual result confirmation, pairwise scores, and earned profile titles. The demo can show two friends, but group claims must work and count in those scores. The exact lock, notification, and title behavior belongs in the PRD.

## Later

Broader social features and richer statistics can follow after the core two-person journey works.

## Explicitly Cut

Cash stakes and payment handling: points are enough to demonstrate a friendly challenge.
Automatic verification of real-world outcomes: friends can settle a result together for this proof of concept.
